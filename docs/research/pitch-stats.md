## The pain (missed calls / no-shows cost real money)

- Average practice misses **23–42%** of inbound calls.
  [AgentZap](https://agentzap.ai/blog/medical-practice-phone-statistics) · [Towards Healthcare market report](https://www.towardshealthcare.com/insights/ai-voice-agents-in-healthcare-market-sizing)
- Each missed call costs **$125–200**; a missed new-patient call is worth **$300–500** in lifetime value.
- Average practice loses **$200K–500K/year** to missed calls; some specialty practices **>$1M/year**.
  [Patient10x](https://www.patient10x.com/content-hub/the-500-000-problem-how-missed-calls-are-destroying-medical-practice-revenue-in-2025) · [Neuwark](https://neuwark.com/blog/missed-patient-calls-cost-medical-practice-revenue)
- **41%** of patient calls come after hours, when there's no front desk staffed at all.
- **74%** of callers switch providers after one bad phone experience.
  [Solutionreach / Keona Health](https://www.solutionreach.com/guide/resources-missed-calls-cost-guide)
- No-shows cost the US healthcare system **$150B/year**; a 5-provider clinic at a 19% no-show rate loses **~$192K/year**.
  [Curogram](https://curogram.com/blog/how-much-each-year-do-no-shows-cost-the-u.s.-healthcare-system)
- **88%** of healthcare appointments are still booked by phone; average call runs **8+ minutes**. Not a shrinking channel.

## The multilingual wedge

- **25M people / 8.6%** of the US are limited-English-proficient (LEP).
  [AHRQ](https://www.ahrq.gov/health-literacy/professional-training/lepguide/chapter1.html)
- State variance is large: **~6%** in Montana/ND/Utah vs **>30%** in Massachusetts, New York, Vermont, West Virginia — gives a concrete "target state" for the demo narrative.
  [KFF/MACPAC](https://www.macpac.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Enrollment-and-Access-Barriers-for-People-with-Limited-English-Proficiency.pdf)
- LEP patients skew Spanish (71.4%), then Vietnamese (3.2%), Chinese (3.0%), Arabic (2.3%).
- LEP patients have documented **higher ED visits and hospitalizations** — language barrier at intake is a clinical-risk factor, not just friction.
- Human phone interpreters cost **$1.25–4/min**; LanguageLine's healthcare line is **$3.95/min**. This is the exact cost line the agent undercuts — a number judges can do live math on.
  [LanguageLine](https://www.languageline.com/personal-interpreter/healthcare)
