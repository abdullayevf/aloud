# Naming candidates

Not chosen yet — pick one and update `README.md` title + `docs/PRD.md` working-name placeholder.

The emotional core to hit: a sick or worried caller, dreading not being understood, gets answered instantly, in their own language, by something calm and competent. The name should land that feeling, not just describe "voice AI for clinics."

1. **Habla** — Spanish for "speak." Short, imperative, warm — an open invitation rather than a feature description. Doubles as an easy brand word for English speakers to say and remember.
2. **Oye** — Spanish "hey" / "listen." Punchy, one syllable-feeling, attention-getting in the way answering the phone is. Slightly more casual/startup-y than "Habla."
3. **Understood** — English, double meaning: the transcription is correct *and* the patient feels cared for. Strong emotional hit for an English-speaking judge panel. **Caution:** a nonprofit (Understood.org, learning differences) already uses this name — fine for a hackathon demo, worth checking before going further with it commercially.
4. **FirstVoice** — evokes being the first calm, competent voice a scared caller hears, independent of language. Reads cleanly in English, no translation-pun dependency.

Leaning recommendation: **Habla** for the pitch — it's the one that's warm *and* instantly legible to a judge panel that may not speak Spanish, and it directly signals the multilingual premise without needing explanation.
